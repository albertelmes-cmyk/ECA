The Process

Concept to
Hardware.

01 / ARCHITECTURE

System Architecture & Specification

We translate your product vision into a robust technical roadmap. This includes component selection with an eye on the UK supply chain, power budget analysis, and defining regulatory requirements (UKCA/CE) from day one.

02 / VALIDATION

Functional Validation & POC

Rapid functional prototyping to validate critical sensor interfaces and power paths. We use off-the-shelf development platforms and custom 'breadboard' PCBs to de-risk the project before committing to full-scale design.

03 / ENGINEERING

Schematic Design & Engineering

Developing detailed schematics using KiCad. Our focus is on signal integrity, low-noise analogue paths, and industrial-grade reliability, ensuring every component is optimized for performance and longevity.

04 / TOPOLOGY

PCB Design

Expert multi-layer PCB layout with strict adherence to IPC standards. We handle complex constraints including impedance control, thermal management, and compact form factors for wearable or industrial IoT devices.

05 / PRODUCTION

DFM & Production Support

Seamless transition to mass production. We provide Design for Manufacture (DFM) reviews, generate full manufacturing data (Gerbers, BOM, XY), and support you through EMC pre-compliance and UKCA/CE certification.
