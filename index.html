PCB Design & Layout

Multi-layer high-speed digital and analogue layout from schematic capture to manufacturing files. We optimise for signal integrity, thermal management, and DFM (Design for Manufacture) to ensure your hardware is ready for UK-based or offshore production at scale.

Wireless Integration

Expert integration of wireless modules and application antennas for robust connectivity. We specialise in layout optimisation, ground plane design, and matching network implementation for Bluetooth LE, Wi-Fi, and LoRa—ensuring your product meets UKCA and CE signal requirements without the guesswork.
