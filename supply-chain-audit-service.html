Is your hardware a "black box"?

In an era of unpredictable silicon cycles and geopolitical shifts, a single End-of-Life notice can halt a production line indefinitely. Legacy designs and unoptimised BOMs are silent liabilities — creating a compounding risk profile that grows with every passing quarter.
