What is ECA Rapid

(01)

The fastest way to a working prototype.

Investors need to see hardware that works. Kickstarter backers need to believe in your product. ECA Rapid is a streamlined prototyping service focused on delivering a functional, demonstrable electronics MVP — quickly and without unnecessary overhead.

We take your concept, scope it ruthlessly to the minimum viable hardware, and build it. The result is a real, working board — not a render, not a mock-up — ready to put in front of people that matter.

How It Works

(02)

Four steps to hardware.

A focused, no-waste process designed to move fast without cutting corners on the things that count.

01
Brief & Scope
Day 1–2

You tell us what the product does, what it needs to demonstrate, and who it's for. We define the minimal feature set — stripping everything that doesn't serve the demo. A fixed scope keeps the timeline tight and the build focused.

02
Design
Week 1–2

Schematic capture, component selection, and PCB layout. We design for speed-to-demo rather than volume production — choosing proven parts, keeping the board compact, and flagging any decisions that could limit future scaling.

03
Build & Bring-up
Week 2–3

Boards are fabricated, assembled, and brought up in our lab. We write the minimal firmware needed to demonstrate core functionality — sensor reads, connectivity, display output, or whatever the demo demands.

04
Handover
Week 3–4

You receive the working prototype along with all design files, a clear summary of what the hardware does, and notes on the path from MVP to production. Ready to demo, present, or pitch on day one.
